Privacy Policy

This website is the personal CV of Edgar Andrés Delgado Castellano (“I”, “me”), based in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. This policy explains what personal information the site collects and how it is handled, following the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What I collect

Only what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email address and message. The site does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies, and it does not create user accounts.

Why I collect it

Solely to read and reply to your message, for example about a job opportunity or a professional enquiry. I will not add you to a mailing list, sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree (Formspree, Inc., United States) receives the form and forwards it to my email. Your message is therefore stored on servers outside Australia. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts this site and may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) serves the typefaces; your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Gmail (Google LLC) is where I receive and answer messages.

How long I keep it

I keep messages only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them when they are no longer needed — or sooner if you ask.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent me at any time. Email andresd.7878@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” from the address you used, and I will act on it within 30 days. I will also ask Formspree to remove your submission.

Automated decisions

This site does not make any automated decisions about you.

Questions or complaints

Contact me at the email above. If you are not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Content and licences

All text and photographs on this site are my own. Typefaces Instrument Serif and Inter are used under the SIL Open Font License.
